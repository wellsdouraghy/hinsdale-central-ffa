    Hinsdale Central

    Made by

    A small crew of friends and a few tireless AI builders: gameplay, world and visuals were built from scratch.

    Campus

    Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Aerial imagery © Esri, Maxar, Earthstar Geographics. Building heights and roof shapes referenced from Google 3D map data.

    Art & audio

    Weapons, effects, outfits, jets and helicopter are procedural and original. All sound is synthesized live with Web Audio. Soldier base model: Mixamo via three.js examples. Sky: Poly Haven (CC0). Textures: ambientCG & Poly Haven (CC0).

    Tech

    three.js, three-mesh-bvh, postprocessing, n8ao, 3d-tiles-renderer, PeerJS. Fonts: Barlow Condensed & Inter (OFL).

      Proximity chat

      Proximity chat is required in this lobby

      Allow microphone access to join. Your voice is always on in matches and other players hear you based on how close you are.

      • Chrome / Edge: click the microphone or lock icon in the address bar → Allow, then Retry.
      • Safari: Safari → Settings for This Website → Microphone → Allow.
      • Firefox: click the microphone icon in the address bar and remove the block.
      • On macOS also check System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone for your browser.
