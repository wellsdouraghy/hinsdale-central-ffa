Proximity chat

Proximity chat is required in this lobby

Allow microphone access to join. Your voice is always on in matches and other players hear you based on how close you are.

  • Chrome / Edge: click the microphone or lock icon in the address bar → Allow, then Retry.
  • Safari: Safari → Settings for This Website → Microphone → Allow.
  • Firefox: click the microphone icon in the address bar and remove the block.
  • On macOS also check System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone for your browser.
